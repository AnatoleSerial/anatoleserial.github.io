LANCER Homebrew

In the far distant future, after ecological collapse and what can be defined as "getting their shit together", humanity has expanded into the stars. Twice, even -- the first time to escape the aforementioned collapse. The galactic hegemon, known as Union, is in its third form of government since its inception 5,000 years ago -- and one that aims for utopian ideals of equality and prosperity. In theory.

Of course, the fringes don't get the same equality and prosperity, but the effort remains. When conflict arises and the usual means of resolution fail, the cavalry comes in: Exceptional Pilots, riding highly personalized Mechanized Chassis, built with cutting edge technology of the Corpro States that coexist with Union.

They are Lancers. The Best of the Best.

Also, the players characters in Massif Press's LANCER, a Tabletop Roleplaying game with an absolute banger of a setting, and crunchy mech combat systems. It's fun as heck, and supported by a large community of GMs and Players, and of course, Homebrewers.

I just might be one of those. SRL Heavy Industries is my repo of Lancer Homebrew. Some is WIP, lot of it untested. Thread with caution.
