## Introduction

Running **LANCER** is challenging. Over at Pilot NET, almost every day there is at least one message, from a prospective Game Master, who is asking other GMs for help on how to run the game. They always get a lot of assistance, because in general GMs love to help. This is a good thing. On the same channel, GMs of varying experience show their work, trade anecdotes and tips on how to run certain types of encounters, and so on.

I have read a lot of what Lancer GMs have to say. A lot of their prep methods, the things they are anxious about, the things they struggle with. It is a lot. But, I have also noticed some common *refrains*, things that many GMs say and/or do, that make me think, *doesn't this kinda SUCK in some way*?

For example, many GMs spend a lot of time preparing combats. They prepare a map, and some enemies, all in fine detail, with the full expectation that all this material will be used in the session. If the combat is not literally the first thing that happens on the session, though, there is always a high chance that the Players will decided the Fighting is not the answer, and there goes all your prep for the day. Maybe you'll be able to reuse that encounter later, maybe not, but it certainly makes it feel like the prep was not worth it.

ANd the opposite happens too: the GM comes prepared for a full Narrative session, but the players have decided that Mech combat is The Way, so now you're stuck in a kinda difficult situation in which you have to improvise something that requires quite a bit of work. If you reuse a previous map, will it fit the narrative? will the OPFOR and Sitrep fit and follow naturally after the narrative?

That's what the first batch of ideas in this Garage are all about: how to do better LANCER prep, and how to run with funnier, less wasteful prep.
