## What is a "LANCER Garage"?

Traditionally, a "Garage" is either a place where vehicles are sheltered, or a commercial business in which vehicles are repaired. The word seems thematic to LANCER itself, having mechs and all, but in truth, for this instance, it comes from another source. In **Vampire The Requiem**'s "Requiem Chronicler's Guide", many writers, some who were or eventually become well-known TTRPG authors and developers, explored how to add or remove elements from a Vampire game to create more unique and interesting games. It is a beautiful book, if anything because it shows a blueprint on how much you can break a game for the sake of fun. The section that broke the game the most was called *Monster Garage*, written by Jared Sorensen, and it's fantastic. It dystills and subverts a lot of what you would expect from a Vampire: The Requiem game, turning it less from a game of monsters clinging to their humanity and more into a lean, mean game of monsters reveling in their inhumanity. Kinda like "The Lost Boys", but more *gothic*. It's stuck with me all these years.

So *my* LANCER Garage is not like that "Monster Garage", but more like the book in which it appeared. Still, the sentiment is there: in this place I plan to break down LANCER's mechanical assumptions and paradigms, tinkering with new ideas to make playing the game more fun for everyone.
