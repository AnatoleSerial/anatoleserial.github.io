### On Neil Gaiman

I remember when the alegations came out. At first you stare in disbelief: this person, whose work, both literary and charitably, you admire, might not be sucha nice person after all. But then again, *who is*?

But, his work, doesn't change. It can remain *inspiring*. You wonder how to react, how to feel, about this whole damn fucking thing.

*Heartbreak* is a good word. A good start.

Mostly because you can't just straight up say, "There is no way Neil Gaiman did not do those things!", when there's multiple people coming forward, when there's a clear *pattern of behavior*. It's not "that one time he screwed up \**teehee*\*". It's multiple times, to multiple people.

I always remember the first days of #MeToo. When George Takei was [accused of sexual misconduct](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/general-news/george-takei-accused-sexually-assaulting-model-1981-1056698/). By one guy. One person. Who couldn't quite remember the encounter and [changed his story](https://observer.com/2018/05/george-takei-accuser-scott-brunton-changed-his-story-of-drugs-assault/):

Abusers in general follow *patterns*. There's never a single accusation.

So when multiple women came forward, accusing Neil Gaiman of sexual misconduct, what should one do?

**Believe them.**

But, then, what?
